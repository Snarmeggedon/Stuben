# Stuben Privacy Policy

Stuben is a personal, self-hosted email assistant used only by its owner.

- **Access:** it requests the read-only Gmail scope (`gmail.readonly`). It cannot send, delete or modify mail.
- **What is read:** sender, subject, headers, snippet and attachment names for recent messages. Mail judged suspicious or junk is never opened further.
- **Storage:** results are written only to files on the owner's own computer. The OAuth token stays local.
- **Sharing:** no Gmail data is sent to, sold to, or shared with any third party, and none is used for advertising or model training.
- **Revoking access:** remove Stuben at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions and delete the local `gmail_token.json`.
